Hi! I'm Dr. Yaniv Proselkov.

Researcher & Strategist  ·  AI Developer  ·  Public Speaker

I work across AI, distributed systems, supply chain finance, innovation ecosystems, and education modelling. CTO at AIOS and Director of Research at Global Cambridge, with a PhD from the University of Cambridge (Institute for Manufacturing, Christ's College). I also deliver lectures, workshops, and full courses to executives, postgraduates, and researchers. On this site you can find my academic papers, think-tank pieces, biography, teaching portfolio, and selected venture work.
