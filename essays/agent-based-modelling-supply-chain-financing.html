Agent-Based Modelling of Firm-to-Firm Supply Chain Financing

27 April 2023

During our Supply Chain AI Lab (SCAIL) research visit to the Complexity Science Hub Vienna, I presented our ongoing research into agent-based modelling of firm-to-firm supply chain financing.

Supply networks operate as complex adaptive systems where financial distress at a single supplier node can ripple through multiple tiers, creating systemic liquidity bottlenecks. Traditional financial models evaluate firms in isolation; by contrast, our multi-agent architecture models how capital flow, payment terms, and cooperative financing mechanisms dynamically propagate across interconnected supply tiers.

It was a privilege to engage with the incredible complexity science community in Vienna, exchanging insights on systemic risk, network viability, and distributed economic control. We look forward to ongoing research collaborations across these frontiers.
